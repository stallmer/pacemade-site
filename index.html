Run plans, made yours.

Build your own structured running workouts on your iPhone, then send them to your Apple Watch. Intervals, tempo runs, long runs, and the pace targets to match, exactly how you planned them.

Pacemade is in development and is not on the App Store yet. Questions? hello@pacemade.app

How it works

  1. Build a planAdd workouts with a warm-up, repeating intervals, and a cool-down. Set each step by time or distance.
  2. Send it to your wristOne tap schedules your workouts in the Workout app on your Apple Watch.
  3. RunYour watch cues you through every step, so you can leave your phone in your pocket.

Made for runners who like to plan

  • Structured workoutsWarm-ups, repeating blocks of work and recovery, and cool-downs.
  • Pace and heart rate targetsYour watch alerts you when you drift out of the range you set.
  • Whole plans, not single runsPlan weeks ahead. Edit a plan and replace it on your watch without duplicates.
  • Move or remove with a swipeSee everything scheduled on your watch and change what you need.
  • Bring your own AI assistantCopy ready-made instructions into any assistant, then paste its plan back. Pacemade has no AI of its own and sends nothing to one.
  • Private by designNo account and no tracking. Your plans stay on your devices.

A look inside

No account. No tracking.

Pacemade does not collect your personal information. Your plans are saved on your iPhone and go to your Apple Watch only when you choose. Read the privacy policy, or find answers on the support page.
