Privacy Policy

Summary

Pacemade lets you build structured running workouts on your iPhone and send them to your Apple Watch. There are no accounts, and we do not collect your personal data. Your workout plans stay on your devices.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information. Pacemade has no account system, no servers that receive your data, and does not require you to sign in.

Data on your device

  • Workout plans. The plans you create or import are saved on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are sent to your Apple Watch only when you tap Send.
  • Backups. If you back up your iPhone, for example with iCloud or a computer, Apple includes app data such as your saved plans in that backup. That backup is governed by Apple's terms, not ours.
  • Exporting plans. If you export a plan with the share button, it goes wherever you choose in the iOS share sheet, such as AirDrop, Files, or Mail. We do not receive it.

Apple Watch and Health

Pacemade asks iOS for permission to schedule workouts on your Apple Watch. You can change that permission in Settings.

Pacemade does not read your Health data, your location, your contacts, or your photos. When you run a workout on your watch, Apple's Workout app records it and stores it in Apple Health under your control and Apple's terms. Pacemade does not receive it.

Making a plan with an AI assistant

Pacemade does not include an AI and does not connect to one. The "Make a plan with AI" screen only copies instructions to your clipboard so that you can paste them into an AI assistant you choose.

When you paste an assistant's reply into Pacemade, the app reads only the text you choose to paste, using the system Paste button. If you share details about yourself with an AI assistant, that company's privacy policy applies to what you share. Pacemade does not send anything to it.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

Pacemade does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it does not contain third-party code that collects data. We do not share data with anyone because we do not collect any.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. We receive nothing otherwise.

Apple services

Pacemade uses Apple technologies such as WorkoutKit. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those services: apple.com/legal/privacy.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as visitors' IP addresses, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not run any analytics or logging of our own. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Children

Pacemade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can delete any plan inside the app, and you can remove scheduled workouts from your watch on the "On watch" screen. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can revoke Pacemade's permission to schedule workouts at any time in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
